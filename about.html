About North Texas

Who We Are

The North Texas State is one of the state bodies of the Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International, uniting churches across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex and North Central Texas under State Bishop Gregory L. Drake.

Our Story

Built on a Legacy.
Moving Forward in Faith.

The North Texas State was established to bring together Full Gospel Baptist churches across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex and North Central Texas — pastors and congregations who hold to Baptist doctrine while walking in the liberty and power of the Holy Spirit.

For many years the State was shepherded by Bishop Kenneth B. Spears, Senior Pastor of First Saint John Cathedral in Fort Worth, whose faithful leadership laid the foundation on which we stand today.

Today the State is led by Bishop Gregory L. Drake, Senior Pastor of The Jubilee Church in DeSoto, under the regional covering of Bishop David C. Cooper. Organized into the Dallas County and Tarrant County Districts, the North Texas State continues to grow — equipping leaders, strengthening churches, and serving our communities with the full Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Legacy & Covering

Those Who Lead Us

Honoring the bishop who built the foundation of the North Texas State, and the regional bishop who covers us today.

Former State Bishop • In Memoriam

Bishop Kenneth B. Spears

Senior Pastor of First Saint John Cathedral in Fort Worth and former State Bishop of North Texas. A beloved shepherd and bridge‑builder in Southeast Fort Worth, Bishop Spears led the State with wisdom, compassion, and a heart for unity. His legacy lives on in the pastors he mentored and the churches he strengthened.

Bishop David C. Cooper

Regional Bishop

Bishop David C. Cooper

Bishop David C. Cooper serves as Regional Bishop within the Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International, providing episcopal covering, counsel, and accountability to the North Texas State and its State Bishop. As a member of the Council of Bishops, he helps connect our State to the wider vision of the Fellowship.

How We Serve

The Full Gospel Pillars

Beyond doctrine, the Fellowship equips every congregation through four pillars of practical ministry.

Faith — Christian Education

Year-round training, credentialing support, and leadership development for pastors, leaders, and laypersons — equipping every believer to be rooted, grounded, and prepared for kingdom impact.

Family — Strengthening Relationships

Biblical teaching, counseling, and connection-building resources for marriages, singles, and families in crisis. We believe strong families build strong communities.

Fitness — Health & Wellness

Through the Health & Wellness Ministry and the national ChurchFit initiative, we equip congregations with nutrition education, fitness programs, and mental-health support — healthy in body, mind, and spirit.

Finance — Economic Empowerment

Biblical principles for budgeting, saving, and legacy planning, helping pastors, leaders, and families build long-term, generational wealth.

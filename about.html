About Us

Who We Are

The North Texas State is one of the regional bodies of the Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International, uniting churches across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex and North Central Texas.

Our Heritage

Rooted in the Fellowship

The Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International was founded in 1994 under the leadership of Bishop Paul S. Morton, Sr., of New Orleans, Louisiana — born out of a desire to give Baptist believers the freedom to embrace the full operation of the Holy Spirit while remaining firmly rooted in Baptist doctrine and heritage.

Today the Fellowship is a global movement organized into states and regions, each led by a State Bishop, with District Overseers providing care and accountability to local pastors. The Fellowship is led by International Presiding Bishop Joseph W. Walker III.

The Fellowship’s motto — “Where Everybody Is Somebody” — reflects its commitment to the dignity and calling of every believer.

Our Foundation

What We Believe

We affirm the historic doctrines of the Christian faith as expressed in the Baptist tradition, together with a full embrace of the ministry of the Holy Spirit.

  • The TrinityOne God eternally existing in three persons — Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • The ScripturesThe Bible is the inspired, authoritative, and infallible Word of God.
  • SalvationSalvation is by grace through faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ.
  • Baptism & CommunionBeliever’s baptism by immersion and the Lord’s Supper as ordinances of the Church.
  • The Holy SpiritThe baptism of the Holy Spirit, with the gifts of the Spirit active in the Church today.
  • Divine HealingGod heals today — spirit, soul, and body — according to His Word.
  • The ChurchThe body of Christ, called to worship, discipleship, fellowship, and evangelism.
  • The Second ComingThe personal, visible return of Jesus Christ and the resurrection of the saints.

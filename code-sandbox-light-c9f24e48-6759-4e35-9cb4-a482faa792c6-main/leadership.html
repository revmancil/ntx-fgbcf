State Bishop

Bishop Gregory L. Drake

State Bishop, North Texas • Senior Pastor, The Jubilee Church

Bishop Gregory L. Drake serves as the State Bishop of the North Texas State of the Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International, providing spiritual covering and oversight to the pastors and churches of the Dallas County and Tarrant County Districts.

He is the Senior Pastor of The Jubilee Church (Jubilee Community Church) in DeSoto, Texas, where he leads a growing congregation committed to worship, the Word, and community impact. Bishop Drake is a sought‑after preacher and teacher whose ministry emphasizes kingdom purpose, spiritual maturity, and developing leaders for the next generation.

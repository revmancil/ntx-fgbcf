Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International

The Fellowship

A worldwide movement of Baptist churches walking in the liberty of the Holy Spirit — “Where Everybody Is Somebody.”

Who We Are

Baptist in Heritage.
Full Gospel in Expression.

The Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship International (FGBCF) is a fellowship of churches, pastors, and believers who hold to the historic Baptist faith while embracing the full Gospel — the complete ministry of the Holy Spirit, including the gifts of the Spirit, in the life of the believer and the local church.

The Fellowship was established to give Baptist believers a spiritual home where they could remain faithful to their Baptist roots and, at the same time, walk in the freedom, power, and liberty of the Holy Spirit without compromise or apology.

Today the Fellowship includes member churches across the United States and around the world, organized into states and regions under the oversight of the Presiding Bishop and the College of Bishops.

Our Story

A Brief History

From a vision birthed in New Orleans to an international fellowship of churches.

The Vision (early 1990s)Bishop Paul S. Morton, Sr., pastor of Greater St. Stephen Full Gospel Baptist Church in New Orleans, sensed a call to unite Baptist believers hungry for the fullness of the Holy Spirit.
The Founding (1994)The Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship was formally organized, and the first International Conference gathered thousands of pastors and believers in New Orleans, Louisiana.
Growth & StructureThe Fellowship adopted an episcopal structure — Presiding Bishop, College of Bishops, State Bishops, and District Overseers — to provide covering and accountability.
A New Season (2015)Bishop Morton passed the mantle to Bishop Joseph W. Walker III, Senior Pastor of Mt. Zion Baptist Church in Nashville, Tennessee, who now serves as International Presiding Bishop.
TodayThe Fellowship continues to grow across the United States and abroad, hosting an annual International Conference and equipping churches for kingdom impact — including the churches of the North Texas State.

What Makes Us Full Gospel

Our Distinctives

The Fellowship holds firmly to Baptist doctrine while embracing the full, present‑day ministry of the Holy Spirit.

Baptist Doctrine

Salvation by grace through faith, believer’s baptism by immersion, the priesthood of all believers, and the authority of Scripture.

The Full Gospel

The baptism of the Holy Spirit and the operation of all the gifts of the Spirit for the Church today.

Liberty in Worship

Freedom for expressive, Spirit‑led praise and worship that honors God and welcomes His presence.

Covering & Accountability

An episcopal structure providing pastors with spiritual covering, mentorship, and relational accountability.

Everybody Is Somebody

Every believer — regardless of background, gender, or status — has value, gifting, and a place in ministry.

Kingdom Impact

Local churches empowered to transform their communities through evangelism, discipleship, and compassionate service.

How the Fellowship Is Organized

Leadership Structure

The Fellowship follows an episcopal model of governance. Local churches retain their autonomy while receiving spiritual covering and support through a clearly defined chain of leadership.

International Presiding BishopChief spiritual leader of the Fellowship
College of Bishops & General BoardBishops and leaders governing the Fellowship’s affairs
State BishopsOversight of churches within a state or region — e.g., Bishop Gregory L. Drake, North Texas
District OverseersPastoral care for churches within each district
Pastors & Local ChurchesAutonomous congregations in covenant fellowship

North Texas State

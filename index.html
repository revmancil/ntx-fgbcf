A Word of Welcome

Bishop Gregory L. Drake

State Bishop, North Texas • Senior Pastor, The Jubilee Church, DeSoto

Welcome to the North Texas State of the Full Gospel Baptist Church Fellowship. We are a family of pastors, leaders, and congregations committed to preaching the full Gospel of Jesus Christ, equipping believers for ministry, and serving our communities with excellence.

Whether you are looking for a church home, seeking fellowship with like‑minded leaders, or simply want to learn more about who we are — you are somebody, and there is a place for you here.

“Where everybody is somebody, and Jesus Christ is Lord.”
Meet Our Leadership

Who We Are

Baptist in Doctrine.
Full Gospel in Power.

We embrace the historic Baptist faith while welcoming the full operation of the gifts of the Holy Spirit. Learn more about the Fellowship

The Word

The Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — our foundation for faith, doctrine, and daily living.

The Spirit

The baptism of the Holy Spirit and the active operation of spiritual gifts in the Church today.

Worship

Liberty in worship — expressive, reverent praise that honors God and invites His presence.

Fellowship

Pastors and churches walking together in covenant relationship, accountability, and support.

Equipping

Training and developing leaders, ministers, and laity for effective ministry in the local church.

Community

Serving North Texas through outreach, compassion, and a visible witness of Christ’s love.

Our Two Districts

One State. Two Districts.

The North Texas State is organized into the Dallas County District and the Tarrant County District, each led by a District Overseer.
